A New Mexico business, selling online

Geico Community LLC works in e-commerce: consumer products, sold to people rather than to businesses, and delivered by post rather than over a counter.

The catalog is built around things used at home and outside it — kitchen tools, patio goods, everyday items worth replacing once and keeping. Everything ships from New Mexico.

The shop is small on purpose. When something is out of stock or an order goes sideways, you write to a person here and get a straight answer.
